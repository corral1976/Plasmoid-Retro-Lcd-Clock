====================================================================
     RELOJ LCD RETRO PLASMOID INSTALLATION AND UNINSTALLATION GUIDE
====================================================================

[ INSTALLATION ]
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open a terminal in the folder where you downloaded the .tar.gz file.
2. Run the following command to install it:

kpackagetool6 --type Plasma/Applet --install com-gitlab-corral1976-relojlcd-1.9.1.tar.gz

3. Restart the KDE panel to apply the changes:

systemctl --user restart plasma-plasmashell

4. TERMINAL NOTE: After restarting the panel, the terminal will spit
   out a lot of KDE diagnostic logs and warnings from other widgets.
   This is COMPLETELY NORMAL and harmless. You can safely close the
   terminal now.

5. To add the clock: Right-click on an empty space on your taskbar,
   select "Add Widgets", search for "Reloj LCD Retro" in the list,
   and drag it onto the panel.


[ UNINSTALLATION ]
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. To remove the plasmoid from your system, run:

kpackagetool6 --type Plasma/Applet --remove com.gitlab.corral1976.relojlcd

2. (Optional) To clear any remaining configuration files in your home:

rm -rf ~/.local/share/plasma/plasmoids/com.gitlab.corral1976.relojlcd

3. Restart the KDE panel to refresh the environment:

systemctl --user restart plasma-plasmashell


====================================================================
 HOW TO REMOVE A BROKEN PLASMOID ICON FROM THE PANEL
====================================================================

If after uninstalling the plasmoid you see a KDE icon in your taskbar
showing the message "Error loading QML file: ... com.gitlab.corral1976.relojlcd
does not exist", follow these steps to clean it up:

1. RIGHT-CLICK on an empty space on your taskbar (or directly on
   the error icon).

2. From the context menu, select "Enter Edit Mode". You will notice
   the taskbar appearance changes.

3. Hover your mouse cursor directly over the icon displaying the
   error message.

4. A small floating toolbar will appear above it. Click the "Remove"
   button (usually an "X" or a trash can icon).

5. To finish, exit edit mode by clicking the "Close" button at the
   top of the screen, or simply press the [Esc] key.

Done! The KDE panel will refresh instantly and the error icon will be gone.
